About

The short version.

I am a Senior Research Associate at the Centre for Governance Studies (CGS), where I lead research projects, write policy briefs and help coordinate the Bay of Bengal Conversation, our international conference. I also edit Global Up-To-Date and write regularly for New Age and Bangla dailies including Kaler Kantho, Bangladesh Pratidin, The Daily Ittefaq and Manab Zamin.

Before CGS, I worked in legal communication, public relations and youth programmes with Oxfam and USAID. I studied Peace and Conflict Studies at the University of Dhaka (BSS and MSS), and spent my university years in competitive debating.
