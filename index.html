Roman Uddin researches and writes on human rights, democratic governance, cyber legislation and freedom of expression, and Bangladesh’s place in a shifting regional order. He led the research for a 2025 study with the Clooney Foundation for Justice on how the Digital Security Act was used against journalists, and his commentary appears regularly in New Age and in leading Bangla dailies.

Press Freedom in Bangladesh: Lessons from the Digital Security Act

A joint report by CGS and TrialWatch examining the chilling effect of the 2018 Digital Security Act on journalism, drawing on cases involving hundreds of journalists and identifying lessons for press freedom ahead of the February 2026 general elections.
